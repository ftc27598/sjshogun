PROJECT POLLINATE / V1.0
Printable bee hotel housing by SJ Shogun. Units: millimetres.

FILES
housing.stl: 180 x 160 mm footprint, 150 mm print height.
roof.stl: 200 x 49.3 mm footprint, 200 mm print height.
bee-hotel.scad: editable CAD with assembly and print-part selectors.
blueprints.svg: dimensioned drawings and assembly notes; opens in a browser.
validation.json: results from checks of the exported STL files.

PRINT
Use a printer with a 220 x 220 x 250 mm or larger build volume.
Both STL files are already oriented with their intended build surface at Z=0.
The housing prints closed-back-down, with the entrance pointing upward.
The roof prints on its front gable edge, with the roof depth pointing upward.
Use a 5 mm brim on the roof for adhesion. Do not lay the roof on a sloped face.
Start with a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 5 top/bottom layers,
and 20% infill. The small 3.4 mm bolt holes require short bridges.
Use a light-coloured outdoor-rated filament; follow its maker's temperature
and UV guidance. PETG is a practical starting point in a sheltered location;
ASA needs a suitable enclosed printer. Avoid dark, heat-absorbing surfaces.
Check the slicer's layer preview and your printer's clearances before printing.

ASSEMBLE
Fit the roof's 4.8 mm channels over the housing's 4 mm side walls.
The model allows 0.4 mm clearance on either side of each wall.
Align the holes and secure with four M3 x 16 mm bolts, washers and nuts.
Use the two 4.5 mm rear holes to screw the housing to a sound support;
choose mounting fasteners for the support material. Clear the floor drains.
Inspect the fit and finish before use. Do not force the roof channels.

NESTING MATERIALS
The printed parts form the outer shelter. Add natural nesting reeds with
closed backs, smooth entrances, varied internal diameters around 4-9 mm,
and lengths around 150 mm. Keep them removable for seasonal maintenance.
The visualization shows 48 reeds in a removable bundle; 48 is also the
assumption used by the site's impact calculator. Actual capacity varies.
Do not print plastic nesting tunnels. Bees should nest in natural materials.
Place securely in a rain-sheltered location with morning sun and native flowers.
Inspect and replace nesting materials according to local pollinator guidance.
https://extension.usu.edu/planthealth/research/making-and-managing-wild-bee-hotels

VALIDATION STATUS
The delivered STL meshes are checked for watertightness, consistent winding,
positive volume, a single connected solid, build-plate placement and build-volume
fit. The assembled housing and roof are checked for collisions.
This prototype has NOT yet been physically printed, fit-tested or field-tested.
Mesh validation cannot establish printer-specific fit or bee occupancy.
